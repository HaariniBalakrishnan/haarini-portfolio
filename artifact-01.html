Professional Growth & AI Leadership.

A collaborative assessment of change-management capabilities and readiness to integrate AI and machine learning.

Introduction & description

This assessment was developed collaboratively as part of my AI/ML coursework. Our group examined strengths and development opportunities in change management and AI/ML integration leadership. The findings draw on different members’ experiences in business, technology, and other fields. My own examples come from business development at Arches, where I work across teams and explore ways to improve outreach and tracking workflows.

Objective

Identify capabilities that support effective change and responsible AI adoption, recognize development needs, and communicate these findings in a way that business and technology audiences can use.

Assessment at a glance

This visual summary is adapted from the group's submitted assessment. The themes reflect group findings, not a claim that every example represents my individual experience.

01 / CHANGE MANAGEMENT — STRENGTHS

Adaptability & Flexibility

Experience adjusting to international teams, shifting expectations, and changing work environments.

Problem Solving

Exploring practical solutions to operational, technical, and client-facing challenges.

Organization & Planning

Breaking complex projects into milestones, responsibilities, and actionable steps.

02 / CHANGE MANAGEMENT — GROWTH AREAS

Communication & Articulation

Explain technical ideas clearly to people with different backgrounds.

Stakeholder Engagement

Build confidence, participation, and shared understanding.

Implementing New Technology

Help colleagues adopt useful processes, not just learn new software.

03 / AI & ML LEADERSHIP — STRENGTHS

Technical AI/ML Understanding

The team brings a mix of hands-on modeling experience and emerging business applications.

Responsible AI

Awareness of fairness, transparency, reliability, and protecting information.

Adoption Strategy

Connecting tools to the needs and workflows of the people using them.

04 / AI & ML LEADERSHIP — GROWTH AREAS

Stakeholder Communication

Translate AI benefits, limitations, and risks into accessible language.

Continuous Improvement

Review performance and look for better approaches consistently.

Change Readiness

Support people through new workflows and evolving technologies.

My professional connection

At Arches, I have seen that introducing a new outreach or tracking tool is not only about learning its features. It also means making the process understandable and useful for colleagues. I also see an opportunity to review outreach response rates more consistently and explore how AI could support thoughtful personalization and workload management. These are development goals, not claims that I have already deployed an AI system.

Process

Our group reflected on professional experiences, compared strengths and weaknesses across change management and AI/ML integration leadership, and supported themes with practical examples. I adapted the shared findings into this visual, audience-focused resource, while separating group examples from my own experience.

Tools & technologies used

Collaborative written self-assessment and structured analysis; HTML and CSS for the portfolio presentation. The source text references SQL, Excel dashboards, containers, machine learning model evaluation, and retrieval-augmented generation as examples of team members' work—not tools I personally used to create this artifact.

Value proposition

Unique value: I connect client-facing business experience and cross-cultural collaboration with an emerging understanding of AI adoption and change management.

Relevance: Consulting and AI/ML teams both need people who can identify opportunities, communicate with stakeholders, and help new technology fit real workflows. This assessment makes those strengths and development priorities visible.
